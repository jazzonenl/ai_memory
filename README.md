# AIM — Enterprise AI Memory

### Make AI knowledge compound.

**AIM helps organizations turn valuable AI-assisted knowledge into a reusable company capability.**

## Why AI Memory

AI is increasingly involved in real work — producing decisions, discoveries, corrections, context and know-how.

But much of that value is temporary.

It can remain trapped inside individual chats, agents, tools or model providers, forcing organizations to repeatedly rebuild context and rediscover what they already learned.

**AI Memory is focused on helping useful AI-generated knowledge survive beyond the interaction that created it.**

---

## The idea

```text
AI-assisted work
      ↓
valuable knowledge
      ↓
retain what matters
      ↓
reuse across people and AI systems
      ↓
compound into lasting company value
```

The goal is not to remember everything.

The goal is to make valuable organizational knowledge **persistent, reusable and useful over time**.

---

## What AI Memory is designed to support

### Continuity
Useful knowledge should remain available beyond a single conversation, agent or application.

### Reuse
People and AI systems should be able to build on what the organization has already learned.

### Relevance over time
Knowledge changes. What is useful today should not be confused with what was true yesterday.

### Company control
Valuable organizational knowledge should remain a company capability rather than depend on one model, vendor or application.

### Portability
Useful knowledge should continue to matter as AI tools, models and technology stacks evolve.

---

## Why it matters

As AI performs more work, organizations should retain more of the value created by that work.

AI Memory is intended to help companies:

- reduce repeated explanation and rediscovery;
- preserve valuable decisions, discoveries and know-how;
- maintain continuity across people and AI systems;
- reduce the impact of stale or fragmented knowledge;
- preserve institutional knowledge through technology and organizational change;
- turn more AI-assisted work into lasting organizational value.

---

## A simple example

Enterprise knowledge is not static.

```text
Yesterday:  John prefers blue.
Today:      John prefers red.
```

A useful memory capability should preserve the fact that the preference changed while keeping future work aligned with what matters now.

This is the kind of continuity AI Memory is intended to support at the product level.

---

## What AI Memory is not

AI Memory is not:

- a claim of AI consciousness or subjective experience;
- a biological model of human memory;
- automatic model retraining after every interaction;
- a requirement to retain everything;
- a claim that all AI-generated material automatically becomes intellectual property.

AI Memory is focused on **useful organizational knowledge that can outlive the session, tool, agent, model or individual that created it**.
